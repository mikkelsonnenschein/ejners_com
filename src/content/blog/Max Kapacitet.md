Danish television documentary series (2022), 6 episodes, Danish Broadcasting Corporation (DR)

Crypto, Rebellion and Freedom. For many, crypto is associated with fraud, uncertainty and risk; for others, it represents the future. In MAX KAPACITET, we meet young people struggling to find their place in a new crypto reality. Filled to the brim with dreams, rebellion and idealism, the series’ characters take us through a new world where ideas are big, the risks even bigger, and nothing is quite as it used to be.
