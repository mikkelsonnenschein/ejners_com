Ejner Benedict Seidelin (b. 1992) is a Danish documentary director and a graduate of the National Film School of Denmark. 
He has directed three documentary series for the Danish Broadcasting Corporation (DR): Max Kapacitet (2022), Jul i Højhuset (2017), and Æterens Ægte Originaler (2016). 
His short films include Mira (2021), Guidance of Our Children (2020), and Days I Have Held (2019). 
Jul i Højhuset was nominated for Best Reportage Series at the Copenhagen TV Festival.
